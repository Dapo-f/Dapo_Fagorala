About Me

I'm a frontend developer in training at New Horizons Nigeria, where I also serve as Class Representative. My background spans UI/UX design, game development interest, and building real products people can use.

I approach every interface like a game level — it should feel smooth, responsive, and rewarding to navigate. I care about the gap between "it works" and "it feels right."

Beyond web, I'm building toward game development as a long-term goal, with C# experience and Unity exploration already underway. My ambition is to one day run a small gaming company contributing to Nigeria's tech ecosystem.

I completed a UI/UX Design course on Udemy in 2024 and hold an A-Level in Computer Science from the University of Lagos.
