Utah Rock

A music app designed for booking concerts and buying albums. Covers the full user journey from discovery to checkout, built entirely in Figma.

Tool Figma
Type UI/UX Design
Screens 11 screens

What I designed

Concert booking flow

Browse events by genre and location, view details, and book tickets in a few taps.

Album & singles store

Purchase music directly, with a clean product page and a 3-step checkout flow.

Artist profiles

Dedicated pages for each artist showing their events, discography, and bio.

Wishlist

Save concerts and albums to revisit later — a simple pattern that reduces purchase friction.
